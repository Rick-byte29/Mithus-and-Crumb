A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Before your
first bite.

Do you have eggless options?

Our sample collection includes eggless sweets and custom cake options. For a real order, confirm ingredients and availability directly with your chosen bakery.

How early should I plan a celebration cake?

For this demo, we suggest 48–72 hours for a simple cake and a week for a detailed celebration design. The enquiry planner lets you put together a sample request.

Can I customise a gift box?

Yes, the concept includes mixed mithai boxes, bakery assortments and personal notes. Explore the celebration planner to preview your gift request.

What about allergens and dietary needs?

These sample products may contain wheat, dairy, nuts and eggs. Always check individual ingredients and cross-contact risks with the bakery before ordering.
